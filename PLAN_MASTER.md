# Master plan (from 2026-09-28)

The single execution plan. Design detail lives in PLAN_AGENCY_V2.md (agency) and E_PLACE_CONCEPT.md (place). Where the code, data and compute live is in INFRASTRUCTURE.md. Work happens on `main`.

## What the model is for

A modular, data-disciplined heterogeneous-agent model of multidimensional wellbeing, in the Beyond-GDP / WISE sense, that others can apply to their own countries and questions. It rests on three properties:

1. **Clean reductions.** Each dimension is a switch, and switching it off gives back exactly the economy without it.
2. **Calibration per configuration.** Every configuration is calibrated to its own targets. G's targets always hold, and S's are added when S is on.
3. **Discipline and transparency.** Every input comes from an official or peer-reviewed source by a scripted route. What is and is not identified is stated, with ranges where the data cannot pin a number.

## Goals and how each is judged

| goal | judged by |
|---|---|
| G1 **Clean inputs** | Every number that enters a solve is classed verified, derived or declared assumption; none unverified. The country table is regenerated by script |
| G2 **A as a real dimension** | Agency has four parts. Capability: alpha from pay data. Security: protection if hit (headline), the expected loss, the job-loss risk. Room to manoeuvre: liquid buffer in months of income. Preference: dread of exposure, from expectations-based news utility (version 2). Plus exact reduction when switched off, and validation of the implied cost of insecurity against the wellbeing literature |
| G3 **The model works well numerically** | Suite passes. Convergence rows move headline numbers by less than tolerance. Every policy economy has a unique equilibrium. CI green. Untargeted moments reported: consumption drop on job loss, MPC out of a windfall, wealth distribution |
| G4 **Modularity** | Each new switch (dread, room-to-manoeuvre reporting, later place) reduces exactly, is tested in the suite, and every configuration is recalibrated |
| G5 **Usable by the WISE audience** | Model outputs mapped to WISE / How's Life indicators. Distribution by group in every output. A scenario notebook. Country cards with borrowed inputs flagged. A sustainability cost side |
| G6 **E built right, later** | Three channels. omega by place measured from predetermined infrastructure. Local social feedback, national financing. Out-of-sample test across countries. Built only on the recalibrated S+G+A |

## Decisions taken in advance, so the work does not stop overnight

- **Inputs:**
  - education shares per country (2015, lfsa_pgaed);
  - alpha from SES 2014 hourly pay, with mean 1 at population shares;
  - B from EU-SILC 2015 (ilc_scp15), with mean 1;
  - effort target and e_ref equal to the HETUS 2010 paid share of committed time for the employed, per country;
  - median-to-mean income per country (ilc_di03, 2015);
  - income process rho = 0.92, eta = 0.10 for all three countries (Bayer and Juessen 2012);
  - unemployed participation ratio: Germany 0.546 (Freiwilligensurvey 2014), France 0.56 (INSEE 1327, volunteering among members, derived) with 0.486 as a sensitivity, Italy 0.937, US 0.857.
- **Dread (version 2).** Expectations-based news utility (Koszegi and Rabin 2009; Pagel 2017), for employed households:

  \[ D(a', z) = \eta_n \, (\lambda - 1)\, \delta_g (1 - \delta_g) \,[\,u(R a' + y_{ref}(z)) - u(R a' + b(z))\,] \]

  with loss aversion lambda from the meta-analysis of Brown, Imai, Vieider and Camerer (2024, JEL) and the news weight eta_n from Pagel (2017), both taken from the literature. **No new calibrated parameter.** The cost it implies, as a consumption equivalent, is checked against Carr and Chung (2014). The term is on when A is on and off otherwise, and eta_n = 0 reproduces today's model exactly.
- **Equilibrium selection.** The highest stable equilibrium, as now. The technology band uses the selected equilibrium only.
- **No hand-tuning.** A configuration that cannot hit its targets is reported as not calibrated. The US stays uncalibrated.
- **Compute:**
  - calibrations, policy tests and the suite run on GitHub Actions (free, parallel, native Linux, Julia 1.7.2 pinned for consistency within this round);
  - the laptop only runs short tests, under 30 minutes;
  - the native Julia upgrade is a separate step after this round, with a reference comparison.
- **Data:**
  - new raw downloads go into a new release (data-2026.10), never overwriting data-2026.09;
  - HFCS never.

## Gates and fallbacks

| gate | pass | if it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 1 inputs | none unverified | keep the previous value, flag it, continue; block only if it is a target |
| 2 dread parameters | lambda and eta_n verified in the published text | use lambda = 2 and eta_n = 1 (the Koszegi-Rabin convention), flagged, and still validate against Carr and Chung |
| 3 dread behaviour | exact reduction; convergence; no mass at the top of the grid; implied cost within a factor of 3 of the wellbeing evidence | version 1: the wellbeing overlay only, with the same weight |
| 4 calibration | own targets within tolerance | not calibrated, reported |
| cloud | jobs finish within 6 hours | split the stages or resume from checkpoints; laptop sessions only with the user's go |
