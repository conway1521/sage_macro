(4) THE PRICE OF THE FABRIC: model-internal against external
  model's own shadow price            33.1 % of GDP per unit of participation
  breakeven for the work subsidy      27.4 % of GDP (subsidy is GDP-B neutral here)
  external, linear WELLBY method     108.8 % of income  (2.510 * 13,000 / 30,000)
  external, ES method                259.6 % of income  (exp(2.510/1.96) - 1)
  external at s = 0.25, linear        27.2 % of income

  The external price equals the breakeven at a mapping strength of s = 0.25.
  Above that the work subsidy is GDP-B reducing; below it, GDP-B improving.
  The model's own price sat only a few points above its own breakeven, which is
  why the paper hedges the sign; the external anchor clears it by a factor of
  4.0 at s = 1 and still clears it at any s above 0.25.
