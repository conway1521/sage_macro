A1. LINEARISATION IDENTITY: amplification against 1/(1 - G')
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

response divided by 1 - G'(r*). Curvature makes large shocks depart from it,
so the test is the LIMIT as the shock shrinks, not the value at tau = 0.20.
map traced at ngrid = 1601 for this test, since at small tau the equilibrium
moves only a few grid steps and a coarse trace would measure the grid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

question that matters is whether the CONCLUSIONS move with it, so propagate
